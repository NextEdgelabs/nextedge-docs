An implementable requirement explains the user, outcome, rule, and verification. Screens alone do not explain a business process.

## Definition of ready

- User and outcome are clear.
- Actors, tenant boundaries, and permissions are identified.
- Inputs, validation, transitions, and failures are described.
- Data ownership, dependencies, and sample records are available.
- Acceptance criteria cover normal and important exception paths.
- Scope and the person who can answer business questions are recorded.
- Change requests and requests for new changes are recorded through support@nextlabsonline.com; the originating email/ticket reference is linked to the work item.
- For out-of-scope work, written Founder/Product Owner approval covers the scope, financial impact, and billing terms; customer agreement is recorded before implementation.

## Example: approve an expense

| Scenario | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Authorized approver accepts a submitted claim | Status changes once; approver, time, and decision are recorded |
| Employee tries to approve their own claim without permission | Rejected; claim remains unchanged |
| Approval is retried after a timeout | No second payment or approval event |
| Receipt or required detail is missing | Correction reason is visible; claim remains reviewable |
| Claim is approved but unpaid | Approval and payment status remain distinct |

Actual approval permissions and eligibility come from the approved project rules.

## Verify at the right level

| Check | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| Business-rule/unit checks | Calculations, eligibility, transitions |
| API/integration checks | Authorization, validation, persistence, transactions |
| End-to-end checks | A user can complete the process across screens and services |
| Concurrency and retry checks | Repeated or overlapping actions preserve invariants |
| Usability and accessibility review | Users can navigate and recover from errors |
| Operational checks | Logs, alerts, migrations, and recovery support the release |

Select coverage by risk. Stock, money, rewards, permissions, and bulk changes deserve explicit failure and concurrency scenarios.

## Definition of done

- Acceptance criteria are demonstrated.
- Relevant checks pass; a reviewer has assessed the change.
- Authorization, tenant isolation, and data integrity are verified.
- Loading, empty, failure, and retry states are covered where relevant.
- Migrations and compatibility impacts are understood.
- Documentation, support notes, and recovery guidance are updated.
- Open defects and accepted risks have owners.

QA supplies evidence and a recommendation. The authorized release owner decides whether remaining risk is acceptable under project release rules.
