Everyone follows the [shared onboarding journey](/onboarding/journey). Learn the project's [delivery method and workflow](/delivery/delivery-methods), including how support interrupts planned work. Confirm your responsibilities through the [role guide](/company/roles-and-responsibilities) and your manager. A role's learning path does not grant approval authority or production access.

Review your [daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly responsibilities](/company/operating-rhythm) with your manager and use actual project work for the first review.

## Developer

Read the [architecture](/engineering/architecture), [data guidance](/engineering/data-and-mongodb), [API boundaries](/engineering/api-and-modules), and assigned domain guide. Trace a request from UI through authorization and business logic to persisted state.

For web work, learn rendering and cache boundaries. For mobile work, learn connectivity, retries, device permissions, and client compatibility through the [web/mobile guide](/engineering/web-and-mobile). Use the framework documented by the assigned project.

**First contribution:** a bounded implementation with relevant checks, Senior Developer review, and clear support notes.

## Senior Developer

Review the project's architecture decisions, critical data invariants, technical debt, release process, and support history. Learn how estimates feed the delivery plan and how recovery authority is assigned.

**First contribution:** a technical approach and reviewed implementation that addresses normal behavior, exceptions, permission checks, migration, and recovery. Pair with a Developer and explain the tradeoffs.

## Product Owner/Manager

Read the [requirement and handoff workflow](/delivery/requirements-and-handoffs), [quality criteria](/delivery/requirements-and-quality), [support cycle](/support/cycle-and-sla), and [change control](/company/ways-of-working#handle-a-change-in-scope).

Learn that Founder/CEO, SVP, and VP are the final requirement/commercial approval authorities. Learn how their recorded decision becomes a versioned document, design reference, tickets, and delivery plan. Understand the difference between backlog ownership and commercial approval.

**First contribution:** turn a recorded finalized requirement into a clear baseline and ready tickets with owners, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and decision links.

## Technical Operations

Walk through an actual operating process in a safe environment. Identify users, source records, rules, exceptions, integration dependencies, and operational impact. Shadow support triage, testing/UAT sign-off, a delivery readiness review, and the Tech Ops release authorization process.

**First contribution:** a workflow map and requirement input package, plus a reproducible support ticket with correct priority context and timestamps.

## UI/UX/Product Designer

Study the users, devices, domain vocabulary, design system, and approved requirement baseline. Review a workflow with Technical Operations and feasibility with Senior Developer.

**First contribution:** a versioned user flow and implementation-ready design covering normal, empty, loading, error, permission, and recovery states where relevant; review the built result.

## Senior Manager (Tech/Ops)

Review client commitments, project scope, delivery capacity, open requirements, support priorities, and escalation contacts. Technical Operations, Product Owner/Manager, UI/UX/Product Designer, Senior Developer, and Developer report directly to Senior Manager (Tech/Ops). Product and design are peers. The Senior Manager reports to the designated Founder/CEO, SVP, or VP. Confirm the leadership decision route, accountable role holders, and approved backups.

**First contribution:** a coordinated delivery/support overview with clear owners, current risks, decisions required, client update plan, and a verified handoff.

## Assigned verification and release responsibilities

Tech Ops owns testing, UAT, and release/recovery sign-off. If assigned to these duties, confirm your named authority and backup, then learn the relevant runbook. Senior Developer and Senior Manager (Tech/Ops) provide technical recovery help. Verification should cover roles, state transitions, data integrity, integration failures, retries, and concurrency.

Demonstrate a safe staging verification or release/recovery exercise before taking on the corresponding production responsibility. These assignments preserve the required approval and review boundaries. Learn the [coverage and exception process](/company/coverage-and-exceptions) before acting as an alternate.
