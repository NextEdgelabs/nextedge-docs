Everyone follows the [shared onboarding journey](/onboarding/journey). Add the path matching your responsibilities. If you cover several functions, agree priorities with your manager.

## Delivery and business analysis

Read the [domain playbooks](/domains/overview), [delivery lifecycle](/delivery/lifecycle), and [requirements guidance](/delivery/requirements-and-quality). Shadow a workflow walkthrough. Document actors, permissions, records, exceptions, and acceptance criteria.

**First contribution:** a ready work item that engineering and QA can implement and verify without guessing the business rule.

## Web and backend engineering

Read the [architecture](/engineering/architecture), [stack decisions](/engineering/technology-stack), [data guidance](/engineering/data-and-mongodb), and [API conventions](/engineering/api-and-modules). Trace one screen action through authorization and business logic to persisted state.

**First contribution:** a bounded, reviewed change with relevant checks and an explanation of its data and permission effects.

## Mobile engineering

Read [web and mobile delivery](/engineering/web-and-mobile), the API contract, and your domain guide. Learn weak-connectivity behavior, permission prompts, retries, local state, and app-version compatibility.

**First contribution:** a complete screen-to-server interaction with loading, failure, and retry states.

The company-wide mobile framework has not been specified in this edition. Use the assigned application's documented stack.

## Quality assurance

Read [requirements and quality](/delivery/requirements-and-quality), [security](/engineering/security-and-access), and your domain guide. Map risks around roles, transitions, data integrity, integration failures, and concurrency.

**First contribution:** evidence for one workflow, including an invalid permission, a repeated request, and a business exception.

## Platform and reliability

Read [releases and incidents](/engineering/releases-and-incidents), the project's environment guide, and [data guidance](/engineering/data-and-mongodb). Understand deployment, monitoring, backup ownership, and recovery.

**First contribution:** a verified staging release or recovery exercise with the procedure updated from observations.

## Growth, support, people, and finance

Read [who we are](/company/overview), [ownership](/company/organization), and [everyday operations](/company/everyday-operations). Learn what your function can approve and which commitments need another owner.

**First contribution:** a complete handoff, reproducible support ticket, customer walkthrough, or administrative workflow appropriate to your role.
